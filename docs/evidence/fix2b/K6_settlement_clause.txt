### Settlement Clause & Payer Confirmation Rules:
1. **Payer Self-Confirmation Prevention:** `request.auth.uid != resource.data.fromUid` required on settlement update when confirming/rejecting (payer cannot confirm their own settlement).
2. **Receiver & Organizer Authorization:** Receiver (`toUid == request.auth.uid`) or organizer (`request.auth.uid == get(/databases/$(database)/documents/trips/$(tripId)).data.organizerId`) are authorized to update settlement status (`confirmed` or `rejected`).
3. **Immutability of Settlement Core Fields:** Amount, `fromUid`, and `toUid` cannot be modified after creation.
4. **Timestamp Enforcement:** `resolvedAt == request.time` required upon settlement resolution.
