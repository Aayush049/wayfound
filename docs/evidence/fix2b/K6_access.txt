### Access Call Matrix (Phase 6 & Settlements / Leaving):
- **Trips Read:** Member only (`request.auth != uid in resource.data.memberIds`).
- **Trips Join:** Non-member joining via valid joinCode, incrementing memberCount by 1, adding caller to memberIds.
- **Trips Leave:** Member leaving by removing own UID from memberIds, decrementing memberCount by 1, updating `members/{uid}` status to `"left"`, ensuring organizer cannot be removed.
- **Settlements Read:** Trip member allowed.
- **Settlements Create:** Member (`fromUid == request.auth.uid`, `toUid` is trip member, amount > 0, status pending).
- **Settlements Update:** Receiver or organizer (not payer) can confirm or reject; payer cannot confirm own settlement.
- **Settlements Delete:** Payer can delete only while status is pending.
