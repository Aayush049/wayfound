1. BALANCE CALCULATIONS & SUM-TO-ZERO INVARIANT
================================================================================
- Location: lib/features/expenses/domain/balance_calculator.dart (computeBalances)
- Logic:
  1. All active trip members are initialized to 0 net balance.
  2. For each expense:
     - Payer (paidBy) gets +amount (credited).
     - Each participant gets -share (debited) computed via splitEqually.
  3. For each confirmed settlement:
     - Payer (fromUid) gets +amount (credited, reducing debt / increasing credit).
     - Receiver (toUid) gets -amount (debited, reducing credit / increasing debt).
- Sum-to-Zero Invariant:
  Because every expense distributes the exact total amount `amount` among participants (`sum(shares) == amount`) and every confirmed settlement transfers `amount` from `fromUid` to `toUid`, the sum of all net balances across all members in `computeBalances` is mathematically invariant and strictly sums to zero ($\sum_{m \in M} \text{balance}(m) = 0$).

2. SETTLEMENT RULES & SIGN CONVENTION
================================================================================
- Location:
  - Domain: lib/features/settlements/domain/settlement.dart
  - Firestore Rules: firestore.rules (match /trips/{tripId}/settlements/{settlementId})
- Sign Convention:
  - Positive balance (`balance > 0`): Member is a creditor (is owed money).
  - Negative balance (`balance < 0`): Member is a debtor (owes money).
  - Zero balance: Member is fully settled.
- Settlement Lifecycle & Firestore Validation:
  - Create: Initiated by payer (`fromUid == request.auth.uid`), receiver (`toUid`) must be a trip member and `toUid != request.auth.uid` (no self-settlements). `amount` must be between 1 and 100,000,000,000 minor units. Status must be `'pending'`, `createdAt == request.time`, `resolvedAt == null`.
  - Update (Resolve): Status transitions from `'pending'` to `'confirmed'` or `'rejected'`. `resolvedAt == request.time`. Authorized only if `request.auth.uid == resource.data.toUid` (receiver) OR `request.auth.uid == organizerId && resource.data.fromUid != request.auth.uid` (trip organizer can confirm settlements sent to other members).
  - Delete (Cancel): Payer (`request.auth.uid == resource.data.fromUid`) can cancel pending settlements by deleting them.

3. SUGGESTED PAYMENTS RECOMPUTATION & GREEDY SETTLEMENT ALGORITHM
================================================================================
- Location: lib/features/expenses/domain/balance_calculator.dart (suggestSettlements)
- Algorithm:
  1. Copies current net balances.
  2. Greedily pairs the largest debtor (`balance < 0`, most negative) with the largest creditor (`balance > 0`, most positive).
  3. Deterministic tie-breaking by UID ascending (`entry.key.compareTo(...) < 0`).
  4. Payment amount is `min(debtAmount.abs(), creditAmount)`.
  5. Updates working balances and repeats until no debtors or creditors remain.
  6. Returns at most `(non-zero balance members - 1)` minimal payments.

  - Creating a settlement for a partial amount leaves a residual non-zero balance, which is correctly reflected in recomputed balances and suggested payments.

5. LEAVE CONSTRAINTS & ELIGIBILITY
================================================================================
- Location: lib/features/settlements/domain/leave_eligibility.dart (checkLeaveEligibility)
- Constraints:
  1. Organizer Cannot Leave: `uid == organizerId` returns denied ('Organizers cannot leave the trip. You can archive the trip instead.').
  2. Zero Balance Constraint: `balance != 0` returns denied ('Cannot leave while you have a nonzero balance... Please settle up or confirm pending payments first.').
  3. No Pending Settlements Constraint: Any pending settlement where `s.fromUid == uid || s.toUid == uid` returns denied ('Cannot leave while you have pending settlements...').
  4. Atomic Execution: `tripRepository.leaveTrip` performs an atomic batch update removing `userUid` from `memberIds`, decrementing `memberCount`, and setting member status to `'left'`. Firestore security rules enforce all leave constraints.

7. MONEY.TRYPARSEMINORUNITS & JPY PER-CURRENCY DECIMALS
================================================================================
- Location:
  - lib/core/money.dart
  - lib/features/trips/domain/currencies.dart
- Per-Currency Decimals & Minor Units:
  - Standard currencies (USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD, CHF, AED): 2 minor unit digits (multiplier 100).
  - JPY (Japanese Yen): 0 minor unit digits (`minorUnitDigits: 0`, multiplier 1).
- `parseMinorUnits` / `tryParseMinorUnits`:
  - For JPY (`digits == 0`), decimal input (e.g. `"100.50"`) throws `FormatException` ("JPY does not support decimal amounts.") unless decimal part is zero (`.0` / `.00`). Integer parsing is used directly.
  - For 2-digit currencies, input with up to 2 decimal places is correctly scaled by 100. Inputs with > 2 decimal places throw `FormatException`.
  - Negative amounts throw `FormatException` ("Amount cannot be negative.").
  - Values exceeding `kMaxMinorUnitsCap` (100,000,000,000 minor units) throw limit exceptions.
  - `tryParseMinorUnits` catches `FormatException` and returns `(int? value, String? error)`.
